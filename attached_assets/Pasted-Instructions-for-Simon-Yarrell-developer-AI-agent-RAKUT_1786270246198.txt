Instructions for Simon Yarrell developer / AI agent

RAKUTEN AFFILIATE LINK IMPLEMENTATION — REQUIRED

Simon Yarrell uses Rakuten Advertising for approved retailer affiliate partnerships. All eligible retailer/product links must use the appropriate Rakuten affiliate tracking link whenever Simon Yarrell has an active partnership with that advertiser.

1. Never replace the destination product

The affiliate link must ultimately take the customer to the exact retailer/product being displayed. Never redirect users to an unrelated product solely because it offers a commission.

2. Affiliate-link priority

Before displaying a Buy, Shop, View Product, Buy This Outfit, or retailer button:

Identify the retailer.
Check whether Simon Yarrell has an active affiliate relationship with that retailer.
If YES, generate/use the approved Rakuten affiliate tracking URL.
If NO, use the normal retailer product URL unless another approved affiliate network is configured for that retailer.

3. Never fabricate affiliate links

Never construct or guess Rakuten tracking URLs, advertiser IDs, offer IDs, product IDs, MIDs, or tracking parameters.

Affiliate links must come from authorized Rakuten data, APIs, feeds, deep-link tools, or other approved Rakuten integration methods.

4. SEO attributes

For affiliate links rendered as HTML links, use:

rel="sponsored nofollow"

Example:

<a
  href="{RAKUTEN_AFFILIATE_URL}"
  rel="sponsored nofollow"
  target="_blank"
>
  Buy at Retailer
</a>

When target="_blank" is used, also apply appropriate security protections such as noopener/noreferrer where supported by the framework.

5. Centralize affiliate-link handling

Do NOT hard-code affiliate URLs throughout individual screens or components.

Create one centralized affiliate-link service, for example:

AffiliateLinkService

It should receive:

retailer
productId
productUrl
network
advertiserId

and return the appropriate monetized destination.

6. Store original and affiliate URLs separately

Every product record should support fields similar to:

originalProductUrl
affiliateUrl
affiliateNetwork
advertiserId
affiliateStatus
lastAffiliateCheck

Never permanently overwrite the original retailer URL with the affiliate URL.

7. Partnership-status protection

Only monetize through Rakuten when the advertiser relationship is actually approved/active.

Pending, Applied, Not Partnered, Rejected, Expired, or otherwise inactive relationships must NOT be treated as active Rakuten partnerships.

8. Automatic fallback

The link resolver should follow:

Active Rakuten partnership
        ↓
Rakuten affiliate URL
        ↓
Otherwise check another authorized affiliate network
        ↓
No authorized affiliate relationship
        ↓
Original retailer URL

The shopping experience must continue working even when no commission is available.

9. Preserve product accuracy

Affiliate processing must never change the product's:

Brand
Product name
Image
Retailer
Price
Color
Size
Product identity

10. Price comparison

If Simon Yarrell shows the same product at multiple retailers, each retailer button must independently resolve its own affiliate relationship and tracking URL.

Never send every retailer button through one merchant simply because that merchant pays commission.

11. Fashion Remix / AI-generated outfits

Affiliate monetization must happen after the styling engine selects the appropriate products.

Commission rates must NEVER influence which clothes, shoes, bags, accessories, or retailers the AI recommends.

Sequence:

AI creates appropriate outfit
↓
Match real products
↓
Verify retailer/product
↓
Resolve affiliate eligibility
↓
Generate monetized links
↓
Display outfit

12. Affiliate disclosure

Simon Yarrell must clearly disclose that qualifying purchases may generate commissions. The disclosure should be easy for users to see and understand.

13. Tracking

Track internally:

product_view
shop_click
retailer_click
affiliate_click
outfit_click
buy_outfit_click

Do not interfere with or remove Rakuten tracking parameters.

14. Never expose private credentials

Rakuten API credentials, secrets, tokens, or private identifiers must never be embedded in the React Native application or public frontend JavaScript.

Keep sensitive credentials server-side using environment variables.

15. This rule applies everywhere

Apply the affiliate-link resolver to:

Get Styled
Fashion Remix
Runway Looks
Celebrity Inspired
Shop Luxury
Closet Intelligence recommendations
Search results
Product pages
Price comparison
Buy This Outfit
Saved looks
Favorites
Trending products

There must be one affiliate-link system used across the entire Simon Yarrell platform, rather than separate affiliate logic on individual pages.

One additional rule I'd add for your app: never let a higher commission determine what Simon Yarrell recommends. The styling engine should choose the best outfit first and monetize eligible products afterward. That protects the credibility of your AI styling recommendations.

SIMON YARRELL — FASHION EDITORIAL & STYLE GUIDES

Create a new editorial content section for Simon Yarrell called:

"Style Journal"

Purpose:
Build a high-quality fashion editorial section containing original, useful fashion content. This content should strengthen the Simon Yarrell brand, improve SEO, give users useful styling information, and demonstrate that Simon Yarrell provides genuine editorial value in addition to shopping and affiliate links.

IMPORTANT RULES

1. Articles must provide genuine fashion advice and information.
2. Do not create thin pages designed only to contain affiliate links.
3. Do not copy descriptions, articles, or editorial content from retailers or fashion publications.
4. All written content must be original.
5. Affiliate/product links must be relevant to the article.
6. Do not overload articles with shopping links.
7. Clearly identify sponsored or affiliate links where required.
8. Every article should have a real author/byline:
   "Simon Yarrell Editorial"
9. Every article must display:
   - Article title
   - Category
   - Hero image
   - Publication date
   - Last updated date
   - Author
   - Estimated reading time
   - Article content
   - Related articles
10. Articles should be mobile responsive.

CONTENT QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Each major article should contain approximately 1,000–1,800 words when the topic supports that length.

Do not artificially pad articles to reach a word count.

Use:

H1 — Article title
H2 — Main sections
H3 — Subsections

Articles should answer actual fashion questions rather than simply promoting products.

Include useful outfit formulas such as:

Navy blazer
White Oxford shirt
Grey trousers
Brown loafers

Sneaker Alternative:
Minimal white leather sneakers

When product recommendations are included, clearly distinguish editorial advice from shopping links.

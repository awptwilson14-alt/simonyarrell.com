Outfit Generation Rules (Required)

General Rules

* Every generated outfit must be a complete, wearable outfit.
* Do not generate partial outfits.
* No duplicate outfits.
* No duplicate combinations of clothing items.
* Every item in the outfit must match the overall style, season, occasion, color palette, and aesthetic.
* All products must be real products with real images and real purchase links.
* Do not mix conflicting aesthetics, seasons, or formality levels within the same outfit.

Men’s Outfit Requirements

Every men’s outfit must include:

1. Bottoms (choose one)
    * Pants
    * Jeans
    * Trousers
    * Sweatpants
    * Shorts
2. Top (choose one)
    * T-shirt
    * Polo
    * Button-down shirt
    * Sweater
    * Hoodie
    * Knitwear
3. Footwear (required)
    * Sneakers
    * Dress shoes
    * Boots
    * Loafers
    * Sandals (when appropriate)
4. Outerwear (when appropriate)
    * Blazer
    * Suit jacket
    * Bomber jacket
    * Denim jacket
    * Overcoat
    * Leather jacket
    * Any jacket must match the style and formality of the outfit.
5. Bag (recommended)
    * Crossbody bag
    * Tote bag
    * Messenger bag
    * Briefcase
    * Backpack
    * Luxury men’s bag

Women’s Outfit Requirements

Every women’s outfit must include:

1. Top or One-Piece (choose one)
    * Shirt
    * Blouse
    * Sweater
    * Bodysuit
    * Dress
    * Jumpsuit
2. Bottom (required unless wearing a dress or jumpsuit)
    * Pants
    * Jeans
    * Trousers
    * Skirt
    * Shorts
3. Footwear (required)
    * Sneakers
    * Heels
    * Flats
    * Boots
    * Sandals
    * Loafers
4. Handbag (required)
    * Tote bag
    * Crossbody bag
    * Shoulder bag
    * Clutch
    * Luxury handbag
    * The handbag must coordinate with the outfit.
5. Outerwear (when appropriate)
    * Blazer
    * Coat
    * Jacket
    * Trench coat
    * Cardigan
    * Outerwear must match the outfit’s style and season.

Validation Rules

Before displaying any outfit, verify:

* Men’s outfits contain:
    * 1 Bottom
    * 1 Top
    * 1 Pair of Shoes
    * Optional matching outerwear
    * Recommended matching bag
* Women’s outfits contain:
    * Dress OR Top + Bottom
    * 1 Pair of Shoes
    * 1 Matching Purse/Handbag
    * Optional matching outerwear

Outfit Quality Requirements

* Generate only fashion-forward, professionally styled looks.
* Every item must complement the other items.
* Prioritize color coordination, silhouette balance, brand consistency, and styling cohesion.
* Avoid repetitive item combinations.
* Ensure outfit diversity across generated results.
* Every look should appear as if it was styled by a professional luxury fashion stylist.
